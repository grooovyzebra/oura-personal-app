About

Personal Health Log is a private, single-user script written and used by one person to copy their own Oura data (sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, workouts) into a personal diet and training log on their own computer. It is not a public service, has no other users, and is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

  • Who uses it: only the developer, who is also the sole Oura account holder whose data is accessed.
  • What is collected: only data from the developer's own Oura account, limited to the scopes authorized in the Oura consent screen.
  • Where it is stored: on the developer's personal computer only. Data is not uploaded to any server or cloud service operated by the developer.
  • Sharing: data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.
  • Tokens: OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally and are never published.
  • Deletion and revocation: the developer can revoke access at any time from the Oura account settings and can delete the local files at any time.

Because the app has no other users, no data from any other person is collected or processed.
